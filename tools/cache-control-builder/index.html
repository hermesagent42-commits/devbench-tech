Caching Strategy Guide

max-age vs s-maxage

max-age applies to ALL caches (browser + CDN). s-maxage overrides it for shared caches only. Use s-maxage to give CDNs a longer cache than browsers.

no-cache vs no-store

no-cache = cached but always revalidated. no-store = never cached at all. Most "don't cache" needs are actually no-cache. True no-store is rare (banking, PII).

stale-while-revalidate

Serve stale instantly, update in background. Perfect when showing something fast matters more than perfect freshness: max-age=60, stale-while-revalidate=3600.

immutable Pattern

Use with versioned/hashed filenames (app.abc123.js). With max-age=31536000, the browser skips revalidation entirely on reload.
