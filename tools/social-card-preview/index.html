How to use these tags

Copy the generated meta tags into your page's <head>. Each platform scrapes different tags: Twitter uses twitter:*, Facebook & LinkedIn use og:*, and Discord primarily uses Open Graph. Validate with opengraph.xyz or use platform-specific debuggers to test your live URLs.
