Why OKLCH?

OKLCH is perceptually uniform — changing lightness produces visually equal changes across all hues. Unlike HSL where yellow and blue at the same "lightness" look wildly different, OKLCH gives designer-friendly color manipulation. Supported in all modern browsers, it's recommended for design systems and CSS custom properties.
